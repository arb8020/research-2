# so the step checkpoints after row 0; the commit keeps [98, 99] (99 is eos) of [98, 99, 100]
# and finishes A. The checkpoint still enters the ring and is published before the finish frees
# everything, so C, sharing A's first 16 tokens, resumes from it.

  expect polled A [5015,98,99]
  expect free 30
