# Mutable party order and delegated moves

This slice closes Assist, Metronome, Beat Up, Beat Up's temporary condition,
and their five reachable callbacks without introducing a named-move resolver.
The native kernel owns a mutable permutation of fixed team slots. Generated
descriptors then request either a candidate-pool delegation or a party-hit
plan; ordinary move execution owns the selected child invocation and hits.

## Authoritative semantics

The oracle is Pokémon Showdown revision
`6e71625b9e6cffeda268b548ae7f43a7643fc3f0`. The relevant sources are the
resolved Gen 3 move entries in `data/mods/gen3/moves.ts`, inherited Assist and
Metronome callbacks in `data/moves.ts`, and party ordering, `useMoveInner`, and
multihit execution in `sim/battle.ts`, `sim/battle-actions.ts`, and
`sim/pokemon.ts`.

## Native boundary

- `native/gen3_slice_data.c` contains generated move-call pools, Beat Up
  programs and owner bindings, and the generation's non-interacting type.
- `native/pokemon.c` owns party-order mutation, current-party candidate
  enumeration, atomic child invocation, party-hit planning, and dynamic
  multihit execution.
- `native/test_pokemon.c#test_assist_current_party_slot_delegation` proves
  mutable ordering, duplicate weighting, and that fainted, statused, and
  zero-PP teammates remain Assist candidates.
- `native/test_pokemon.c#test_beat_up_party_hit_plan` proves contributor
  filtering, per-contributor stats, dynamic hits, and the zero-contributor
  nullable BasePower result with exact PRNG staging.
- `native/test_pokemon.c#test_generated_move_call_pool_and_atomic_delegation`
  proves generated Metronome selection and rollback-safe delegation.

## Differential basis

The six legal cases generated by
`verify/cases/generate-party-delegation-cases.js` cover Beat Up party order,
status filtering, typeless damage into Ghost, zero contributors, nested
Metronome-to-Assist selection with duplicate weighting, and an empty Assist
pool. Together they contribute 18 observed boundaries and preserve choices,
handler/event ordering, state, requests, and labeled PRNG calls.

Passing these cases supports only the mapped descriptors. It does not claim
all delegated moves or all party-dependent mechanics.
